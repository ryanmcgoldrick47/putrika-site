About

Jakarta-born, Dharawal country-based singer, songwriter, and electronic music producer.

Putrika (born Putri Karina) is a Jakarta-born, Australia-based singer, songwriter, electronic music producer, and selector. Currently based in Dharawal/Illawarra country after spending over a decade living on Gadigal Land (Sydney), she creates a signature sound described as alt-soul electronica. Her music seamlessly blends multi-layered vocals, experimental synths, percussive rhythms, and ambient soundscapes, deeply influenced by both Western electronic music and her traditional Indonesian roots.
